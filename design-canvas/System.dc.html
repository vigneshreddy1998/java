Gold is reserved for the commit action at the end of a form; maroon for navigation and primary actions elsewhere. Selected states borrow the accent of the event you're on, which is why the same control reads green on the Sangeet and maroon on the Wedding.

The arch

The single repeated motif — a pointed arch masking each event card. It carries the celebration's character without leaning on ornament, and it is the one shape a guest will remember from the site.

The mask must carry preserveAspectRatio="none". Without it an SVG mask defaults to meet, letterboxes inside its box, and silently clips whatever sits at the bottom of a card.

Anything that must escape the shape — the floating date chip — is a sibling of the masked element, never a child of it.
